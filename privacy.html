Governance & Compliance

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection Principles

Our community platform collects only minimal information necessary to deliver workshop registrations, conference tickets, and mentorship matching. Information collected includes your name, email address, GitHub handle, and dietary requirements for in-person summits.

2. Use of Information

Attendee details are strictly utilized to coordinate venue access, issue calendar invites, and communicate urgent schedule changes. We never sell, monetize, or distribute participant data to external marketing vendors.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Event registration logs are retained for a maximum of 180 days following event completion. Participants may request complete deletion of their account records by emailing our lead organizers.
